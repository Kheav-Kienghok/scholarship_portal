User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 14, 2025

You can request the deletion of any data this app has collected from your Facebook account.

To delete your user data:

  1. Go to your Facebook account settings.
  2. Navigate to “Apps and Websites”.
  3. Find this app and remove it from the list.
  4. This will revoke all future access to your information and delete any data previously shared with us.

If you would like us to manually delete your data from our systems, please email us at your-email@example.com with your Facebook user ID.
